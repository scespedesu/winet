WiNet is regularly looking for motivated MASc/PhD students, postdocs, and visiting researchers interested in wireless networking, IoT, satellite communications, or intelligent transportation systems.

## Graduate students

If you're interested in joining as a graduate student, please:

1. Look through our [Research]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}) and [Publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}) pages to see if your interests align with the group's.
2. Email **{{ site.contact_email }}** with your CV, transcripts, and a short paragraph on which research direction interests you and why.
3. Note that admission to the graduate program is handled separately through Concordia's [Gina Cody School graduate programs](https://www.concordia.ca/ginacody/programs/graduate.html) — a match with the group does not guarantee admission, and vice versa.

## Postdocs and visiting researchers

Postdoc and visiting-researcher opportunities are usually tied to specific funded projects. Reach out by email with your CV and research interests, and we'll let you know about current openings.

## Undergraduate research

Concordia undergraduate students interested in a research project (e.g., through USRA/independent study) are welcome to reach out directly.
