The **Wireless Networking Research Group (WiNet)** studies wireless and mobile networking and communication protocols, with a focus on making connectivity work in constrained, remote, and heterogeneous environments — from IoT sensors to low-Earth-orbit satellites.

WiNet was founded in 2014 by **Prof. Sandra Céspedes** in the Department of Electrical Engineering at Universidad de Chile. Since 2022, the group is based in the [Department of Computer Science and Software Engineering](https://www.concordia.ca/ginacody/computer-science-software-eng.html), Gina Cody School of Engineering and Computer Science, at **Concordia University**, Montreal, Canada.

*(Our 2014–2022 pages from Universidad de Chile are kept online for reference — see [previous site]({{ '/legacy-uchile/index.html' | relative_url }}).)*

## Current research topics

- Direct-to-satellite IoT and space networking
- Constrained IoT and low-power wide-area networks (LPWAN)
- Heterogeneous and vehicular wireless networking
- Intelligent Transportation Systems: modeling, protocols, and simulation
- 5G/6G radio access networks and congestion control
- Delay-tolerant and interplanetary networking
- Connecting the unconnected
- Internet standardization (IETF)
